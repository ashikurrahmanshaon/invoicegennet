CASH FLOW & SPEED

How to Get Paid 2x Faster: 4 Billing Tactics

Eliminate the friction between completing great work and seeing funds land in your business bank account.

Freelancers and agencies report that chasing overdue invoices consumes up to 15% of their total work week. Implementing structured payment workflows cuts DSO (Days Sales Outstanding) in half:

TACTIC 1
Provide 1-Click Payment Gateways (Stripe & PayPal)

When an invoice merely provides bank wire details, client payment requires logging into commercial banking portals, setting up new beneficiaries, and waiting for OTP verification. By contrast, including a direct Stripe card link or PayPal checkout button enables client finance managers to settle with a single corporate card swipe in under 30 seconds.

TACTIC 2
Bill Within 24 Hours of Milestone Completion

Client enthusiasm peaks the moment work is approved. An invoice delivered within 24 hours of project completion carries emotional momentum and is processed immediately. Waiting until the end of the month risks project details going cold.

TACTIC 3
Shorten Payment Windows to Net 14

Net 30 was invented for analog mail dispatch in the 20th century. In today's digital email environment, Net 14 creates healthy urgency without stressing client accounts payable schedules.

TACTIC 4
Automate Polite, Pre-Due Reminders

Don't wait until an invoice is 10 days late. Send a friendly courtesy reminder 3 business days before the due date stating: "Just sharing a quick reminder that invoice #INV-2026-003 is scheduled for settlement on Friday. Let us know if you need any additional receipts!"
