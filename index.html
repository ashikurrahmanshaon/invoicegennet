Answers to Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about generating, customizing, and downloading professional invoices on Invoice-Gen.net.

Is Invoice-Gen.net really 100% free?

Yes, completely free. You can generate unlimited invoices, customize headers, upload company logos, and download clean vector A4 PDFs without any watermark, fees, or hidden trials.

Do I need to sign up or create an account to download invoices?

No registration is required. You can start typing directly on the page and click "Download PDF" immediately. Signing up is optional and allows you to sync clients and invoices to your private cloud across devices.

Is my billing and client data confidential and secure?

Yes. All invoice PDF generation occurs entirely client-side in your web browser. Your financial figures, client addresses, and banking notes are never saved to our servers unless you explicitly choose to save them in an authenticated account.

Can I include sales tax, VAT, GST, and discounts?

Yes. The editor provides flexible tax inputs (VAT, GST, Sales Tax), percentage or flat discounts, and shipping cost calculations that dynamically update subtotals and balance due in real time.

How do clients pay me from the invoice?

You can paste your direct Stripe Payment link, PayPal.me link, or bank transfer wire information (IBAN, Swift, Routing & Account numbers) directly into the payment instructions area so clients can pay you with one click.
