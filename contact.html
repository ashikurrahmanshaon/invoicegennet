Frequently Asked Support Questions

Where is my invoice data stored?
Your data never leaves your device. Everything is saved locally in your browser's private local storage. No invoice details are sent to external web servers.
Can I request new international currencies?
Yes! If your preferred regional currency or symbol isn't listed on our Currencies Hub, contact us and our developers will add it to the engine.
How does the 14-day refund work?
If you purchase the Pro Plan ($5/mo) or Lifetime Deal ($70) and are not 100% satisfied, simply email billing@invoice-gen.net within 14 days for a full refund.
Can I print or email the generated PDF?
Yes. Invoices are generated as true vector A4 PDFs, meaning they can be attached directly to emails or sent to physical commercial printers with zero pixelation.
