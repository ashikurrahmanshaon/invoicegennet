GST & Tax Invoicing Rules & Compliance

A registered tax invoice must satisfy specific statutory requirements to allow your client to claim input tax credits (ITC, VAT recovery, or GST refunds). Key mandatory fields include your registered Business Tax/GSTIN number, client address, place of supply, sequential serial numbering, and clear itemization of HSN/SAC codes alongside applicable tax rates.

Intra-State GST (India)

Split equally into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST) when supplier and buyer are located within the same state.

Inter-State IGST

Assessed as a single integrated tax when supplying goods or services across state or national boundaries.

UK / European VAT

Requires explicit display of gross taxable amount, VAT rate (20% standard), and unique VAT registration number.
