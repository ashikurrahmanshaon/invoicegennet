Our Story & Vision

In 2016, creating a clean invoice online meant either installing heavy desktop software or signing up for predatory SaaS subscriptions that held customer documents hostage behind monthly paywalls.

Invoice-Gen.net changed that by pioneering an effortless web-first experience: open the site, enter your line items, choose your currency, and immediately download a razor-sharp vector PDF. No forced sign-up, no hidden fees, and zero server logging of sensitive trade details.

Privacy by Design

Your client list, invoice balances, and bank details are computed directly in your browser without ever being uploaded to unauthorized databases.

Instant Vector Rendering

Our PDF layout engine delivers pixel-perfect, crisp vector documents formatted to universal A4 standard for professional printing and emailing.

Modern Gateway Integration

Seamlessly generate invoices pre-configured for Stripe card payments (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and PayPal Express checkout.

Our Core Values

We believe independent creators and small enterprises are the backbone of the modern global digital economy. Our platform exists to protect their time and safeguard their financial autonomy.

  • Speed First: You shouldn't spend more than 60 seconds generating and sending a professional bill.
  • Zero Data Hostage: All your invoice templates and saved state remain under your control in local browser storage.
  • Fair & Transparent: A permanently free tier for everyone, complemented by optional Pro and Lifetime upgrades for teams requiring multi-template branding.
