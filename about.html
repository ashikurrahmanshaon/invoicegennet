About Invoice-Gen.net

Invoice-Gen.net is a modern billing suite built for independent contractors, freelancers, and small business owners who need to generate professional invoices quickly without bloated software or restrictive paywalls.

Why Invoice-Gen.net

Built from the ground up around six core pillars of financial workflow productivity:

Simple Invoicing

Zero complicated setups. Open the web builder, fill in your line items, configure taxes, and produce clean invoices in less than two minutes.

Professional Vector PDFs

Generate high-DPI A4 vector documents with embedded fonts, crisp logos, and exact printable page margins suitable for corporate accounts.

Easy Client Management

Save repeat client profiles, billing contacts, and default payment terms to populate subsequent invoices with a single click.

Useful Tool Suite

Over a dozen integrated productivity tools including tax calculators, live exchange rate conversions, image compressors, and PDF splitters.

Secure Cloud Saving

Optional account integration backed by an isolated relational SQLite database allows persistent invoice history across desktop and mobile devices.

Workflow Efficiency

Instantaneous zero-lag client-side rendering with keyboard shortcuts, draft auto-preservation, and one-click duplicate invoice functionality.

How It Works

A transparent four-step flow that gives you total control over your billing:

1
Create

Open the builder and input your deliverables, unit rates, quantities, and client contact details.

2
Customize

Select your template, upload your logo, set your currency symbol, and configure your payment terms.

3
Save

Keep your invoices stored locally in your browser, or save them to your secure cloud database for anytime access.

4
Download / Send

Export a high-DPI vector A4 PDF with clickable payment links, or share an instant payment link directly.

Product Principles

Simple

We prioritize clear inputs and clean layouts over confusing feature clutter. You can bill a client in seconds.

Professional

Every exported PDF follows legal tax accounting standards and universal A4 vector formatting.

Fast

Zero bloated frameworks and zero server round-trips for drafting. Edits update with 0ms visual latency.

Reliable

Your work is preserved automatically. Work offline without fear of losing your drafts or client contact lists.
