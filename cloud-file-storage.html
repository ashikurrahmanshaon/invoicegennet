Frequently Asked Questions

How does invoice cloud storage work on Invoice-Gen.net?

Whenever you generate an invoice, convert a PDF, or calculate payment schedules, you can save your files directly into your personal vault. Files can be accessed, sorted, renamed, and downloaded anytime.

Are my saved documents private and secure?

Yes. Documents stored on Invoice-Gen.net are isolated per session with strict zero-knowledge security. No third parties have access to your invoices, pricing, or client records.

What file types can I store in the cloud vault?

The storage system supports A4 PDF invoices, JPG and PNG image receipts, CSV installment and numbering schedules, and compressed ZIP archives.

Can I download my files offline anytime?

Yes. Every stored document can be streamed and downloaded directly to your device with standard MIME headers at full original resolution.
