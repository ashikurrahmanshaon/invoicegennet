What to Include on Every Freelance Invoice

To ensure smooth processing and prevent accounting disputes with enterprise or small business clients, always include these critical fields:

1

Clear Contact & Legal Info

Include your full legal or trading name, business email, portfolio or website URL, and your tax registration number (SSN, EIN, or VAT ID).

2

Itemized Deliverables & Timesheets

Instead of generic lines like "Design work", specify: "UI Design Sprint 2: Mobile Checkout Prototype (16 hrs @ $75/hr)". Transparent breakdowns prevent client pushback.

3

Structured Sequential Invoice ID

Use structured numbers like FL-2026-004. This helps client finance departments track records and simplifies your annual tax reconciliation.

4

Clear Settlement Instructions

Include both your electronic payment link (Stripe / PayPal) and direct bank account details (IBAN, Swift, or Routing/Account) in the payment notes section.

Frequently Asked Questions for Freelancers

How do I handle revisions or late fees on invoices?

You can add a brief terms clause in the Notes section, e.g., "Payments past 14 days accrue a 1.5% monthly late assessment. Project scope includes two rounds of revision."

Do I have to charge sales tax or VAT as a freelancer?

This depends on your country of tax residence and whether you exceed statutory VAT/GST thresholds. In InvoiceGen, you can set custom tax percentages from 0% to any standard rate with automatic line calculation.

Can I save my client details so I don't have to re-enter them every month?

Yes! InvoiceGen's client storage stores your client roster in your browser, enabling 1-click invoice generation for recurring monthly retainer clients.
