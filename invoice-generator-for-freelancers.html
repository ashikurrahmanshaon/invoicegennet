Freelancer Invoicing Best Practices

Choose the invoicing model that matches your contract agreement. Hourly billing provides detailed transparency into time expenditures. Milestone billing protects cash flow on large projects by billing upon sprint completions. Retainers and fixed fee agreements ensure steady predictable revenues without time-logging disputes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions about features, formats, privacy, and workflows.

What should a freelance invoice include?

Your contact info, client billing details, invoice number, issue/due dates, itemized tasks, totals, and payment instructions.

Can I bill for both hourly work and fixed milestones on the same invoice?

Yes. You can mix hourly rate entries and fixed-fee project items within the same invoice.

What payment terms should freelancers use?

Net 14 or Net 15 is recommended for freelancers to maintain steady cash flow and avoid lengthy corporate delays.

How do I invoice clients overseas?

Select your client’s currency and provide international wire instructions like IBAN, SWIFT/BIC, or PayPal.

Is this tool free for freelancers?

Yes. It is 100% free with unlimited invoice downloads and zero subscriptions.
