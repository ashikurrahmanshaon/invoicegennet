Frequently Asked Questions About PDF Invoices

Can I save my PDF invoice and edit it again later?

Yes. InvoiceGen automatically saves your draft in your browser's encrypted local storage. When you return to the site, your draft is restored instantly.

Will the PDF open properly on mobile devices?

Absolutely. Because it uses standard vector PDF rendering, your invoice opens smoothly in Apple Books, Adobe Acrobat Reader, Google Drive, and native Android document viewers.

Can I print the PDF without edge cutoff?

Yes. The layout enforces standard 0.75-inch printable margins so no text or totals will be clipped when printed on standard home or office printers.
