Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions about features, formats, privacy, and workflows.

What is an invoice payment link?

A payment link is a secure URL that leads your client straight to a checkout page to settle a specific invoice balance with credit card or PayPal.

Do clients need an account to pay through a link?

No. Clients click the link and immediately pay using their debit card, credit card, Apple Pay, or PayPal without registering.

Can I send payment links through messaging apps?

Yes. Payment links can be pasted into WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, email, Slack, or any messaging platform.

Do payment links expire?

You can configure links to expire upon payment completion or set custom expiration windows for time-sensitive invoices.

Are payment links secure?

Yes. Links use encrypted token parameters to prevent tampering with invoice amounts, currencies, or payee identifiers.
