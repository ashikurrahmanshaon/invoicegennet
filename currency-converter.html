Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions about features, formats, privacy, and workflows.

Where do the live currency exchange rates come from?

Our rates are fetched from verified open exchange rate financial data feeds and cached hourly for optimal speed and reliability.

How often are the exchange rates updated?

Rates are synchronized continuously via our backend API with live UTC timestamps displayed on screen.

Can I convert between any two world currencies?

Yes. You can pair any of the 160+ supported world currencies, such as USD to EUR, GBP to CAD, or AUD to JPY.

Does this calculator include bank conversion fees?

The tool displays mid-market exchange rates. You can adjust the figure to account for your bank’s typical 1-3% foreign exchange spread.

Can I bill in foreign currencies on Invoice-Gen.net?

Yes. Our invoice generator lets you select any major international currency symbol for your PDF invoice.
