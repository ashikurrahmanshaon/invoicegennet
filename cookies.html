Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026. A clear and honest breakdown of the cookies and browser storage mechanisms used by Invoice-Gen.net.

1. Overview and Core Privacy Principle

At Invoice-Gen.net, our foundational architecture is designed around data minimization. We do not sell your personal data, we do not track your browsing across the internet, and we do not utilize third-party advertising tracking networks.

To make the website function properly, remember your billing preferences, and maintain secure user sessions, we use standard HTTP cookies and browser LocalStorage as described below.

2. Cookies Actually Used on Invoice-Gen.net

We classify the cookies used into the following distinct categories:

A. Strictly Essential Cookies

These cookies are technically required for authentication, route protection, and security. Without them, you would not be able to log in or securely manage your cloud invoices.

  • invoicegen_session (Session Token): An encrypted, randomly generated identifier assigned upon successful email or Google login. Sent with HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure flags to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS) or forgery. Expires automatically when your session closes or after 7 days of inactivity.

B. Functional & Preference Cookies

These cookies preserve your customized workspace settings across visits so you do not need to reconfigure them each time you generate an invoice:

  • invoicegen_lang: Remembers your selected language (English, Spanish, French, German, Bengali, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese) for the multi-language UI engine.
  • invoicegen_theme: Preserves your visual interface preferences.

C. Browser Local Storage (Client-Side Storage)

In addition to standard cookies, Invoice-Gen.net uses HTML5 LocalStorage in your web browser. This is what allows guest users to build invoices without an account:

  • invoicegen_draft: Preserves your current in-progress invoice draft (company name, line items, notes) so refreshing or navigating back won't erase your work.
  • invoicegen_invoices: Stores the list of finalized invoices generated locally on your machine when operating as a guest.
  • invoicegen_clients: Stores your saved client billing contacts for quick autofill on future invoices.

3. Third-Party Integrations & Cookies

When you optionally interact with external payment providers or authentication services through our platform, those third parties may set cookies in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Identity Services (GIS): If you sign in using the "Continue with Google" button, Google sets cookies to authenticate your account and securely pass your profile token to our server.
  • Stripe & PayPal Checkout: If you use payment checkout links, Stripe or PayPal handle card verification in compliance with PCI-DSS standards.

We do NOT install third-party tracking pixels (such as Facebook Pixel or invasive behavioral ad cookies).

4. How to Manage or Clear Cookies and Storage

You have full control over the cookies and local storage saved on your computer or mobile device:

  • In Your Browser Settings: You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). Blocking essential cookies will prevent logging into account features, though guest PDF generation remains available.
  • Clearing Invoice Drafts: You can click the "Reset Form" button in the invoice editor at any time to instantly purge all current draft data from your local browser storage.

5. Questions Regarding This Policy

If you have questions about our use of cookies, LocalStorage, or our privacy architecture, please contact our data protection desk:

Email: privacy@invoice-gen.net
