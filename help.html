Popular Help Articles

Do I need an account to create and download invoices?
No. You can open Invoice-Gen.net, enter your line items, and immediately download high-resolution vector A4 PDFs without registering. Accounts are completely optional and are only needed if you want cloud synchronization across devices.
Is my financial data stored on your web servers?
For guests, your invoice draft is stored strictly in your browser's private LocalStorage. When you export a PDF, the rendering happens 100% on your device using client-side libraries. For registered users, data is stored in our isolated SQLite database protected by strict session authentication.
How do sequential invoice numbers work?
Invoice-Gen automatically increments sequential IDs (e.g. INV-001 to INV-002) whenever you finalize an invoice. You can customize the prefix, date token, and starting digit to match your local accounting standard.
Can I bill in foreign currencies?
Yes. We support all major world currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, JPY, INR, BDT, and 40+ others) with configurable currency symbols and automated exchange rate lookups.
