Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions about features, formats, privacy, and workflows.

How do installment payment calculations work?

The calculator splits the principal balance across your designated number of periods, computing uniform periodic payments and remaining balances.

Can I calculate bi-weekly or monthly frequencies?

Yes. You can switch between weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, or custom intervals.

Can I export the repayment schedule to Excel?

Yes. Click "Export CSV" to download the complete payment breakdown for use in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers.

Does this calculator estimate credit card fees?

Yes. You can review gateway fee estimations to understand net proceeds after typical merchant processing charges.

Can I include this payment plan on my invoice?

Yes. You can list the scheduled payment dates and amounts in the Payment Terms section of your Invoice-Gen invoice.
