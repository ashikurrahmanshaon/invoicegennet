Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions about features, formats, privacy, and workflows.

Which image formats can I compress?

You can compress JPG, JPEG, PNG, and modern WEBP images directly.

How much file size reduction can I expect?

Most standard photos and scanned documents achieve between 40% and 80% reduction with no visible quality difference.

Does compression alter image pixel dimensions?

No. Pixel width and height remain identical to the original image unless you choose to downscale.

Are my images secure and private?

Yes. All compression happens locally inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas APIs. Images are never transmitted over the internet.

Is there any watermark on the output?

No. Invoice-Gen.net never adds watermarks, stamps, or advertising to your images.
