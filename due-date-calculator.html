Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions about features, formats, privacy, and workflows.

What does "Net 30" payment terms mean?

"Net 30" indicates that the client is obligated to pay the entire invoice balance within 30 calendar days of the issue date.

What happens if the calculated due date lands on a weekend?

Standard commercial billing rolls the deadline to the following Monday, which our calculator performs automatically when selected.

What is the difference between Net 30 and EOM?

Net 30 counts exactly 30 calendar days from invoice issue. EOM (End of Month) sets the payment deadline on the final day of the billing month.

How should I display the due date on my invoice?

Best practice is to state both the payment term (e.g. Net 30) and the explicit calendar date (e.g. Payment Due: November 15, 2026).

Can I use this calculation in my invoice?

Yes. The calculated due date maps directly to the Due Date field in Invoice-Gen.net.
