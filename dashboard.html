Help & Documentation

Answers to common invoicing questions, workflow tutorials, and direct support access.

How do I download a vector PDF invoice?
When creating or viewing any invoice, click Download PDF. Our engine compiles the document client-side with zero server latency, generating a crystal-clear vector A4 PDF ready for print or email.
How do client auto-population and business defaults work?
In the Business Profile tab, specify your business name, address, tax rate, and terms. Every time you create an invoice, these details auto-populate. When choosing a saved client from your directory, their contact and billing address are instantly filled.
Where can I read invoicing best practices?
Check our editorial guides on The Complete Invoicing Guide and Getting Paid Faster Strategies.
