Help & Documentation

Guides on invoice creation, client autofill, and payment reconciliation.

How does vector PDF download work?
Our engine compiles standard true A4 vector PDFs (210 × 297mm) client-side with 0ms server latency. All inputs, line items, and styling are preserved cleanly across multiple pages.
How do Business Profile and Client Autofill work?
Enter your business profile defaults once under Settings → Business Profile. Every new invoice created will automatically populate your business details. When selecting a client from the dropdown under BILL TO, customer contact info is auto-filled instantly.
How do I record offline or manual payments?
In the Payments tab, click + Record Payment, pick the invoice, enter the wire or cash amount, and the invoice balance due and status are automatically updated.
