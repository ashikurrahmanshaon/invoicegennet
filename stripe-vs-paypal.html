Stripe vs. PayPal: Best Invoicing Gateway

A head-to-head analysis of payment processing fees, client payment friction, and currency conversion rates for professional invoices.

When invoicing clients across North America, Europe, Asia, and Latin America, choosing the right payment processor directly impacts both your profit margin and the speed at which clients approve and settle bills.

Head-to-Head Comparison Matrix

Feature Stripe PayPal
Standard Domestic Fee 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction 2.99% + $0.49 per transaction
Supported Card Brands Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay PayPal Balance, Linked Bank, Major Credit Cards
Client Experience Inline, frictionless card payment directly on your page Redirects or modal popup requiring PayPal authorization
International Currencies 135+ currencies with local clearing rails 25+ major currencies with global brand recognition
Payout Speed 2-business-day rolling bank transfer Instant to PayPal balance; 1-3 days to bank

When to Choose Stripe

Stripe is the undisputed gold standard if you work primarily with B2B corporate clients, startups, and agencies who expect to pay invoices via corporate credit card without having to register a third-party account.

When to Choose PayPal

PayPal is invaluable when invoicing consumer clients, non-profits, or international clients in regions where local credit cards have strict cross-border merchant restrictions. Clients love the familiarity and buyer protection backing their PayPal balance.

The Recommended Hybrid Approach

You don't need to choose! With Invoice-Gen.net, you can include both a Stripe card checkout option and a PayPal button on your bills, giving every client their preferred payment experience.
