How We Tested and Ranked Invoicing Software

To find the best invoice generator in 2026, we tested 12 of the most popular invoicing tools on the market against four essential criteria:

  • Speed & Friction: Can you generate a complete invoice without enduring mandatory email verification or credit card prompts?

2. Invoice Home — Simple Templates with Basic Options

Invoice Home offers a range of colorful pre-made graphics. However, the free tier restricts download volume and places watermarks on documents unless you upgrade to a monthly subscription.

3. Zoho Invoice — Feature-Dense for Small Accounting Teams

Zoho Invoice provides extensive accounting utilities like time-tracking and expense logging. However, the multi-step onboarding, phone verification, and complex interface can feel overwhelming for users who simply need an invoice quickly.

4. Wave Invoicing — Best for Integrated Payroll and Bookkeeping

Wave is a solid bookkeeping suite that includes invoice sending. However, transaction fees are higher, and the tool pushes users toward proprietary payment processing rather than allowing your own Stripe or PayPal credentials.

Key Features to Look for Before Choosing an Invoicing App

1. Unbranded PDFs: Never send a bill to an enterprise client that features the generator's promotional logo. Always choose a tool that exports clean, unbranded documents.

2. Multi-Currency Support: If you work with international clients, ensure your invoice tool supports conversions across USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, and Asian currencies.

3. Tax and Discount Precision: Automated calculation prevents rounding errors and keeps books compliant with tax authorities.
