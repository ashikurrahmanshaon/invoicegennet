Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions about features, formats, privacy, and workflows.

How do I merge PDF files online?

Upload two or more PDF files, arrange them in your desired sequence by dragging the cards, and click "Merge PDFs" to download the combined document.

Does merging PDFs degrade document quality?

No. Our merger retains the original vector typography, embedded fonts, and vector elements at full resolution.

Are my uploaded documents uploaded to a server?

No. The merging algorithm operates entirely within your browser using WebAssembly and PDF.js. Your documents remain strictly private.

Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can combine?

You can combine standard business documents without artificial paywalls or arbitrary limits.

Can I use this tool on a mobile phone?

Yes. The interface is fully responsive on iOS and Android devices, allowing you to merge files on the go.
