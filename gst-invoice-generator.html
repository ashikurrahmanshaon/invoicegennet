Understanding GST & Tax Invoice Requirements

In many jurisdictions, issuing a simple billing slip is insufficient for clients who intend to claim back input tax credits or business expenses. Government revenue services enforce strict legal criteria regarding what constitutes a valid "Tax Invoice".

Using an outdated or non-compliant format can lead to tax audit penalties, rejected deductions, or delayed payments. Invoice-Gen.net is specifically calibrated to handle international tax regimes seamlessly.

Mandatory Elements on a Valid Tax Invoice

According to international tax authorities (such as the Australian Taxation Office and HM Revenue & Customs), an invoice must contain:

  • Official Document Label: The document header must explicitly state "Tax Invoice".
  • Tax Registration Identifier: Your registered ABN, VAT Number, or GSTIN clearly displayed alongside your trading name.
  • Transaction Date: The date the invoice is issued to establish the applicable tax accounting period.
  • Granular Itemization: Detailed breakdown of what was sold, quantity, and whether each item is taxable or tax-exempt.
  • Tax Total & Currency: Distinct line for the exact GST/VAT amount charged, presented in the legal trading currency.

Frequently Asked Questions About GST Invoices

Do I need to charge GST if I am not registered?

No. If your turnover is below your national registration threshold (e.g. $75,000 in Australia or £90,000 in the UK) and you are unregistered, you must NOT charge GST/VAT. In InvoiceGen, you can leave the Tax field at 0%.

Can I include both ABN and bank transfer details?

Yes. You can add your ABN directly in the business address block and include your BSB and Account number or Stripe link in the Payment Notes section.
