What Should an Invoice Include?

To guarantee prompt payment from client finance teams, every professional invoice should contain:

  • Official Header: Clearly labeled "INVOICE" with your trading name and optional company logo.
  • Unique Sequential Number: Non-repeating identification code for accounting tracking.
  • Key Dates: Both the Date of Issue and the definitive Due Date.
  • Line Item Breakdown: Clear deliverable descriptions with quantities, unit rates, and totals.
  • Payment Remittance Instructions: Bank wire details (IBAN/Routing) or clickable Stripe/PayPal payment links.
