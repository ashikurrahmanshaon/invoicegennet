Getting Started with Invoice-Gen

Follow this straightforward 8-step guide to configure your business profile, customize billing terms, and produce publication-grade vector A4 invoices.

1

Choose Your Mode: Instant Guest or Free Account

You can create and export invoices immediately without an account. 100% Free forever, zero registration required, and completely client-side private.

Start Creating an Invoice Now →
2

Enter Your Business Profile & Logo

Add your business or trading name, email, street address, and phone number. Upload your company logo (PNG or SVG) to automatically feature high-resolution branding at the top of your bills.

Open Invoice Editor →
3

Add Client Information

Type your client's contact name, business entity, billing address, and email. Once typed, client details can be saved to your Client Directory for fast one-click reuse on future invoices.

4

Set Invoice Number, Issue Date & Due Date

Assign a unique sequential identifier (e.g. INV-2026-001). Select the issue date and define realistic payment terms such as Due on Receipt, Net 14, or Net 30 calendar days.

Explore Sequential Numbering →
5

Itemize Products & Services

Add clear line item descriptions, hours or quantities, and unit rates. Detailed service descriptions prevent questions from client accounts payable teams and accelerate settlements.

6

Configure Currency, Taxes & Discounts

Choose your billing currency (USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, etc.) and enter your local tax rate (VAT, GST, or Sales Tax). Apply any project milestone discounts. Totals calculate dynamically.

Use Tax Calculator Tool →
7

Provide Payment Instructions or Click-to-Pay Links

Enter your wire transfer account or embed a direct Stripe or PayPal link. Clickable links inside the exported PDF allow clients to settle balances immediately.

Learn Payment Gateway Best Practices →
8

Save & Download Crisp Vector A4 PDF

Click 'Download PDF' to compile your vector document. The generated A4 PDF is completely clean, zero watermarks, and ready to attach to an email or send to commercial printing.

Create Your First Invoice Now →
